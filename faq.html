Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Here are answers to common questions about working with TEMIVANCE. If your question isn't covered, get in touch.

How long does a website project take?

It depends on the page count, features, content readiness and feedback cycle. A focused site may take less time than a complex multi-page or e-commerce build. Your proposal should include an agreed timeline before work starts.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope, design complexity, integrations and content needs. TEMIVANCE provides a tailored quote after understanding the project. Domain registration, paid hosting, premium tools and other third-party fees may be separate.

Do I need to pay everything upfront?

Payment milestones should be agreed in your written proposal before work begins. The exact schedule will depend on the scope and terms agreed for your project.

How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds should be stated clearly in your proposal. Additional rounds or major changes to the agreed scope may affect cost and timeline.

Will my website work on phones?

Responsive layouts are part of the approach: pages should adapt to common phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes. Final testing should cover key devices and browsers.

Can you help me get a domain and hosting?

Yes, we can discuss the options appropriate for your site. Domain names and hosting may have recurring fees. For a simple static website, GitHub Pages can host the site for free, but it is not suitable for every backend or e-commerce requirement.

Can I update the website myself?

That depends on the platform and how the site is built. Static HTML sites can be edited in code; content-management platforms may make routine edits easier. We'll clarify the editing approach during planning.

Will my website guarantee more sales or conversions?

No ethical web designer can guarantee a specific conversion rate or sales result. We focus on clarity, usability, trust signals and easier customer journeys. Results also depend on your offer, audience, traffic quality, pricing and follow-up.

What do you need from me to start?

Helpful starting materials include your business name, services, target customers, goals, brand assets, contact details, preferred examples and any existing website. If you don't have everything yet, we can identify what's needed together.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed and scoped separately. Availability, response expectations and included tasks should be agreed in writing.
